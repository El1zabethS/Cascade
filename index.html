Valuable hardware.
What comes next?

As AI infrastructure is rapidly upgraded, capable GPUs and servers are removed from their original environments while they still have remaining useful life. The challenge is not simply finding someone willing to buy used hardware. It is determining which next user can make effective use of each asset based on its performance, condition, efficiency, location, and market demand.

Without that connection, usable compute can be undervalued, inefficiently redeployed, or prematurely recycled.

MATCHING
PURPOSE

Giving capable hardware a second life by matching its remaining power to a real computing need.

Retired AI hardware does not lose its value simply because it can no longer meet the demands of its original user. By evaluating its capabilities against the actual requirements of a next user, that technology can be placed where it performs effectively without providing unnecessary computing power.

The result is infrastructure that is powerful enough for the job, without the cost, energy use, and waste of more technology than necessary.

WHAT IS
CASCADE?

A two-sided marketplace for retired AI infrastructure.

Cascade connects organizations retiring AI hardware with users whose computing needs match that hardware’s remaining capabilities.

Traditional marketplaces ask: “Who will buy this?”

Cascade asks: “Who is this hardware right for?”

The goal is not to sell more powerful hardware. It is to match the right amount of power to the right user.
