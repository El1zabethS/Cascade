# CASCADE

### Reverse the Future

Cascade is a reverse supply chain marketplace designed to give retired AI hardware its highest value next life.

As hyperscalers, data centers, and AI companies upgrade their infrastructure, capable GPUs, servers, and other computing hardware can become stranded despite having significant remaining useful life. Cascade explores a different approach: matching the capabilities of retired AI hardware with the actual computing requirements of its next user.

Instead of asking **“Who will buy this?”**, Cascade asks:

> **“Who is this hardware right for?”**

## The Problem

AI infrastructure is advancing rapidly. As organizations replace hardware to meet increasingly demanding workloads, older technology can lose value to its original owner long before it loses its ability to perform useful computation.

At the same time, startups, universities, researchers, small businesses, and other organizations may not need or be able to justify the newest and most powerful AI hardware.

This creates a gap between **retired computing capacity** and **demand for affordable computing infrastructure**.

## The Solution

Cascade proposes matching hardware capability to actual computing need.

Rather than assuming every user needs the newest technology, retired hardware can be evaluated against the requirements of potential next users. Hardware can then be directed toward applications where it remains powerful enough to perform effectively without creating unnecessary cost, energy consumption, or excess computing capacity.

**The goal is not maximum computing power. It is appropriate computing power.**

## How Cascade Works

Cascade connects two sides of the AI infrastructure lifecycle:

### Supply

Hardware can enter Cascade from organizations including:

* Hyperscalers
* AI companies
* Data centers
* Enterprises
* Universities

### Cascade

Hardware is evaluated using factors such as:

* Computational performance
* Hardware condition
* Energy efficiency
* Remaining useful life
* Geographic location
* Transportation costs
* Market demand
* Workload requirements

Cascade uses these factors to determine where an asset can create the greatest economic and functional value.

### Demand

Potential next users include:

* Startups
* Small businesses
* Universities
* Researchers
* Schools
* Edge AI applications
* Community computing
* Parts harvesting and recycling

## Business Model

Cascade is designed as a marketplace rather than an inventory heavy hardware reseller.

The platform can generate revenue through transaction fees while connecting sellers directly with appropriate buyers. This allows Cascade to facilitate hardware movement without necessarily purchasing and holding depreciating inventory itself.

Additional revenue opportunities may include:

* Hardware testing and certification
* Logistics coordination
* Secure data erasure
* Lifecycle analytics
* Enterprise asset management

## Prototype

This repository contains an early prototype exploring how the Cascade platform could operate.

The prototype demonstrates the concept of a centralized marketplace where retired AI hardware can be evaluated, matched, and redirected toward appropriate next users.

Future development could expand the prototype to include hardware benchmarking, buyer workload profiles, automated matching scores, lifecycle tracking, pricing recommendations, and environmental impact estimates.

## Why Cascade?

Cascade is not intended to be simply another marketplace for used GPUs.

Traditional marketplaces help sellers find someone willing to purchase an asset. Cascade's long term value proposition is determining **which market, workload, or user represents the best next destination for that asset.**

By improving how AI infrastructure moves between generations of users, Cascade aims to:

* Extend the useful life of AI hardware
* Reduce premature electronic waste
* Lower barriers to accessing AI compute
* Improve utilization of existing infrastructure
* Recover economic value from retired assets
* Reduce unnecessary deployment of overpowered hardware
